# Company

**helpwave GmbH** · Aachen & Münster, Germany · founded 2022

Public pages: [About](https://helpwave.de/about) · [Contact](https://helpwave.de/contact) · [Imprint](https://helpwave.de/imprint) · [Privacy](https://helpwave.de/privacy) · [Legal & infrastructure](https://helpwave.de/legal)

## Mission

Build the open foundation for healthcare infrastructure so clinicians can focus on patient care. Reduce complexity, improve efficiency through continuous analysis, and keep organizations in control — no vendor lock-in.

## Story in brief

- **2022** — Founded in Aachen after Münsterhack; early focus on clinical coordination and open healthcare software.
- **Today** — Open healthcare operations platform for Europe: ambulatory and stationary care, process intelligence, modular products.
- **July 2024** — helpwave became the operator of mediQuu, taking on its healthcare-networking products and the practices, pharmacies and care providers that rely on them, and bringing those users into the open platform.

## Values

- Open source by design
- Healthcare staff first
- Continuous improvement from real usage
- Partnership over product
- Trust through transparency
- Based in Aachen & Münster — built for European healthcare
